01 Our story

Established in Dubai with a vision to connect the world to premium quality goods

Situated in the heart of the UAE, Magrol Global specialises in the dealership of luxury and utility automobiles, prime real estate investments, and high-quality building materials.

Dubai is not an accident of geography for us — it is the reason the business works. The city sits where shipping routes, capital and supply meet, which means a company based here can reach markets and inventory that a company based elsewhere simply cannot.

Our headquarters serves as the central hub for every one of our operations. It is where vehicles are inspected, where property briefs are worked, and where materials are consolidated before they ship. Every product we source and sell meets the highest international standards, because it all passes through one place and one team.

02 How we work

Four commitments we do not negotiate

One

Inspect before we offer

Vehicles are checked for quality and performance before they are listed. You are not the quality control step.

Two

Buy at source

We deal with manufacturers, distributors and owners directly. Fewer hands on a transaction means fewer margins on it.

Three

One point of contact

The same team carries an order from enquiry to handover, across all three divisions.

Four

Say what is true

If a listing is overpriced or a timeline is unrealistic, we say so before you commit, not after.

03 Headquarters

Everything routes through Dubai.

Our Dubai office is the central hub for the group: sourcing, inspection, documentation and export all originate here. Our Ghana office operates as the import arm on the other end of that corridor, handling logistics, customs and final delivery.

Two offices, one chain of custody. That is what lets us stand behind a shipment from the moment it is bought to the moment it is handed over.
