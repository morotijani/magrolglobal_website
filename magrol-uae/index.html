02 Our story

Established in Dubai to connect the world to quality goods

Magrol Global was founded in Dubai with a straightforward premise: the UAE sits at the crossing point of global trade, so a business built here can reach markets that a business built elsewhere cannot.

We specialise in the dealership of luxury and utility automobiles, prime real estate investment, and high-quality building materials. Our Dubai headquarters is the central hub for all of it — every product we source and sell is held to the same international standard.
