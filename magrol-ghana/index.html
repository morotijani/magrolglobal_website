02 The import process

From enquiry to delivery

Below is the route every order takes. Four steps, one point of contact, and no part of the chain subcontracted to someone you have never met.

01

Enquiry & quotation

Tell us what you need — a specific car, an investment brief, a materials schedule. We check availability in Dubai and return a firm quote with a timeline.

02

Purchase & inspection

Our Dubai team buys on your behalf, inspects the goods against the order and prepares export documentation. You are copied on the paperwork before anything leaves the UAE.

03

Shipping & customs

Sea freight or air freight depending on urgency and volume. We clear the shipment through Ghana Customs at Tema Port and handle every document the authorities require.

04

Delivery to your door

A vehicle is driven or low-loadered to you. A materials order is delivered to site. Either way, you receive it where you need it and we stay on the line until you confirm everything is right.

AE Where it all begins

Everything is sourced through Magrol Global Dubai.

Our Dubai headquarters is the sourcing and inspection hub for the entire group. This is where stock is held, where property briefs are worked, and where materials are consolidated for export — so every shipment has a single, traceable chain of custody from the UAE to Ghana.
